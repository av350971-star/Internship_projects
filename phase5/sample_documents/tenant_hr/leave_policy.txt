Comprehensive Leave and Time-Off Policy

1. Casual Leave (CL):
- Full-time employees are credited with 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year (accrued 1 day per month).
- Employees may take a maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days without requiring skip-level manager approval.

2. Sick & Medical Leave (SL):
- Employees receive 10 days of Sick Leave annually for medical recovery.
- If sick leave extends beyond 2 consecutive business days, a verified medical certificate signed by a licensed doctor is strictly mandatory upon return to work.

3. Maternity & Paternity Leave:
- Female employees are entitled to 26 consecutive weeks of fully paid Maternity Leave for up to two surviving children.
- Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of fully paid Paternity Leave, which must be utilized within 6 months following childbirth or adoption.

4. Year-End Carry Forward & Encashment Rules:
- Employees can carry forward a maximum of 8 unused Casual Leave days into the subsequent calendar year.
- Any unused Casual Leave exceeding 8 days automatically lapses on December 31st.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed into cash compensation under any circumstances.
