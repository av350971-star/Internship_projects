Company Employee Handbook & Workplace Guidelines

1. Working Hours & Attendance:
- Official office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday.
- Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all team members must be reachable on Slack.
- Employees are permitted up to 2 days per week of remote work with prior notification to their reporting manager.

2. Probationary Period:
- New joiners undergo a standard probationary period of 3 months from their joining date.
- A formal probation review meeting is scheduled during the 10th week.
- If performance is unsatisfactory or below expectations during probation, the probation period may be extended or employment terminated.
- Upon successful completion, the employee receives a formal confirmation letter.

3. Company Hardware & Equipment:
- Full-time employees are provided with either an Apple MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad based on team requirements.
- Hardware refreshes and upgrades are eligible every 3 years.
- Lost or damaged accessories must be reported immediately to the IT Helpdesk within 24 hours.

4. Code of Conduct:
- Equal opportunity employer with zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment.
- All proprietary source code, internal discussions, and customer data are confidential under the signed NDA.
