# Employee Compensation, Benefits, & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Annual Appraisal Cycle
- The company's annual performance review cycle commences in October each fiscal year.
- Performance ratings and salary revisions become formally effective on April 1st of the following calendar year.

## 2. Performance Bonus Tiers
Annual performance bonuses are distributed according to verified evaluation tiers:
- **Exceptional Performer (Tier 1)**: 15% of annual base CTC.
- **Exceeds Expectations (Tier 2)**: 10% of annual base CTC.
- **Meets Expectations (Tier 3)**: 5% of annual base CTC.
- **Needs Improvement (Tier 4)**: 0% bonus and mandatory enrollment in a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

## 3. Business Travel & Per Diem Reimbursements
- **Domestic Travel**: Economy class airfare is booked via the internal corporate travel portal.
- **Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)**: 1,500 INR per day for Tier-1 metropolitan cities (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad), and 1,000 INR per day for Tier-2 cities.
- All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted with valid GST invoices within 15 days of trip completion.

## 4. Health & Medical Insurance
- Comprehensive group health insurance cover of 500,000 INR sum insured per family unit.
- The policy covers the employee, legally wedded spouse, and up to two dependent children up to 25 years of age.
- Cashless hospitalization is supported at over 6,500 empanelled hospitals across India.
